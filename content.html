✦ Our Process ✦

How We Create

01

Understand the Need

We work closely with the Organizing and Outreach teams to understand what each event requires in terms of medical procedures and volunteer skills.

02

Research & Draft

Our medical student members research best practices and draft accurate, accessible guidelines and training materials for each initiative.

03

Review & Finalize

All content is peer-reviewed internally and refined to ensure it is both clinically sound and easy for all volunteers to follow.

04

Deliver & Update

We distribute content to the team before each event and continuously update our materials based on feedback and evolving best practices.

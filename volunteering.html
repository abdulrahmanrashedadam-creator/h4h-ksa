✦ Our Process ✦

How We Mobilize

01

Spread the Call

We announce upcoming events and volunteer opportunities across campus — through social media, word of mouth, and university channels.

02

Sign Up & Welcome

Interested students sign up and get personally welcomed into the H4H community — added to our WhatsApp group and briefed on what to expect.

03

Prepare for the Event

Before each event, we share the Content team's briefing guides and assign volunteers to specific roles so everyone knows exactly what they'll be doing.

04

Celebrate & Keep Going

After every event, we recognize our volunteers' contributions and keep the momentum going — because H4H's strength is its people.

✦ Our Process ✦

How We Operate

01

Plan the Event

We define the goal, set a date, and map out every detail — from location to headcount to resources needed.

02

Recruit Volunteers

We reach out across the campus, brief volunteers on their roles, and build the team for the day.

03

Execute on the Ground

On event day, we coordinate every moving part — making sure everything runs on time and on plan.

04

Debrief & Improve

After each event we review what worked and what didn't, so the next one is even better.

Hearts For Humanity

Our Story

Helping Hands
for Homeless

H4H (Hearts For Humanity) is a healthcare and medical student-led charitable initiative at Alfaisal University — driven by empathy, clinical knowledge, and a commitment to real community impact.

We run medical education workshops, health screenings, donation drives, and community outreach — serving underprivileged families in shelters, orphanages, and low-income neighborhoods across Saudi Arabia.

H4H operates under Alfaisal University's MSA, uniting skilled healthcare providers with one shared goal: creating a healthier, more dignified environment for the vulnerable.

🤝

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between those who have and those who need — through organized action, genuine empathy, and consistent community presence.

  • ✦ Student-led & passion-driven
  • ✦ Alfaisal University, Riyadh
  • ✦ Led under Alfaisal University's MSA
  • ✦ 100% volunteer-driven operations

Our Activities

What We Do

Four pillars of action — delivered by healthcare students who bring both compassion and clinical expertise to every initiative.

🩺

Medical Education Workshops

We bring healthcare knowledge directly to shelters, orphanages, and low-income schools — running workshops on CPR & First Aid, menstrual health, emergency response, and mental health support.

Learn More
🩻

Health Checkups & Screening

Our medical student volunteers conduct free health screenings for breast & cervical cancer and comorbidities like hypertension and diabetes — reaching communities who have no other access to care.

Learn More
📦

Donation Programs

We collect and distribute medications, clothes, and female hygiene products to underprivileged families — ensuring every person receives support with full dignity and care.

Learn More
🕌

Collaborative Volunteering

We partner with H4H Jeddah for Umrah Medical Camps, collaborate with the SAKINAH group on women's health, and establish community support systems for the underprivileged.

Learn More

Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need any prior experience to volunteer?

+

Not at all. All you need is a willing heart and a few hours of your time. We guide every new volunteer through the process.

How often do events take place?

+

We aim to hold events every 2–3 weeks. You'll receive updates via our WhatsApp group as soon as an event is confirmed.

Is H4H open to students from all majors?

+

Yes! Hearts For Humanity welcomes every Alfaisal student regardless of major, year, or background. Compassion has no prerequisites.

What kind of donations do you accept?

+

We accept clothing, non-perishable food items, hygiene products, and blankets. Monetary donations can also be arranged — contact us for details.

Will I get a volunteer certificate?

+

We are working on issuing participation certificates for active volunteers. Stay tuned for announcements in the WhatsApp group.
